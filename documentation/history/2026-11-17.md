## Decisions

- **eslint-plugin-obsidianmd 0.5.x, no rule lowered.** The preset now reads `manifest.json`, `package.json` and `LICENSE` with their own languages, so `eslint.configs.recommended` and `tseslint.configs.recommended` are scoped to JS and TS files (unscoped, `no-irregular-whitespace` throws on `manifest.json`). The preset switches `no-implied-eval` and `@typescript-eslint/no-misused-promises` off for TS in favour of replacements: both stay at error next to the replacements. The preset no longer encodes `isDesktopOnly` in the severity of `obsidianmd/no-nodejs-modules` (always warn; the rule reads the manifest), so `import/no-nodejs-modules` reads it through the preset's `isManifestDesktopOnly()`.
- **Rule floor raised twice**, each in its own commit: `obsidianmd/no-misused-promises` (error) and `obsidianmd/no-nodejs-modules` (off → warn) with 0.5.1; `no-unassigned-vars`, `no-useless-assignment`, `preserve-caught-error` with ESLint 10. 535 rules.
- **Changelog through the package API, not the CLI.** The conventionalcommits preset 10 refuses the old writer ("requires conventional-changelog-writer@9 or newer"), so PR #7 alone would have stopped the next release at the changelog step; CI does not run that step. The CLI of conventional-changelog 7+ writes into `CHANGELOG.md`, or with `--stdout` prints the whole merged file, so `generateEntry()` in `scripts/generate-changelog.ts` uses `ConventionalChangelog` directly. New spec: the real generator in a throwaway repository.
- **ESLint 10 needed a lockfile refresh**: the hoisted `@typescript-eslint/utils` was held at 8.48.1, which throws "Class extends value undefined" under ESLint 10 (the failure on PR #6). `bun update @typescript-eslint/utils` moved it to 8.72.2.
